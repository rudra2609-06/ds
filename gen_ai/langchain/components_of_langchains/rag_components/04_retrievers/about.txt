Retrievers in langchain are the components which helps us to fetch the relevant documents from data source w.r.t to provided query.

In langchain all Retrievers are runnables which means we can add them in chains

Types of Retrievers (basis of data source):

1.Wikipedia Retrievers
2.Vector Store Based Retrievers
3.Archive Retrievers

Types of Retrievers (basis on Search Stratergy)

1.MMR (Maximal Marginal Relevance) Retriever
2.Multi-query Retriever
3.Contextual Compression Retriever
