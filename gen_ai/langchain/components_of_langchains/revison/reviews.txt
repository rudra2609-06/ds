Review 2: The blender arrived two days late, and the jar was cracked. Customer support sent a replacement quickly, but the replacement makes a loud rattling noise. I am disappointed that I had to contact support twice.

Review 3: The cotton bedsheets feel soft and fit my queen-size mattress. The color is slightly lighter than it looked in the photos, but the quality seems good after two washes.

Review 4: This desk lamp is easy to assemble and has three useful brightness settings. Unfortunately, the touch control sometimes changes brightness by itself, and the charging port stopped working after a week.

Review 5: Delivery was on time. The stainless-steel water bottle keeps drinks cold for hours, does not leak in my backpack, and is easy to clean. I would recommend it.

Review 6: I ordered running shoes in my usual size, but they feel much tighter than expected. The return instructions were confusing, and I have not received an update about my refund. Please make the return process clearer.

Review 7: The notebook is fine for everyday use. The paper is a little thin for marker pens, but regular ballpoint ink does not bleed through. Nothing special, though it does the job.

Review 8: The phone case fits perfectly and has already protected my phone from one fall. The buttons are still easy to press, but the clear plastic started turning yellow after only a few weeks.

Review 9: I like the backpack's many pockets and comfortable straps. It holds my laptop and lunch, but the zipper gets stuck when the main compartment is full. Overall, it is useful but could be better made.

Review 10: The coffee maker is simple to use and brews quickly. The coffee tastes good, but the water tank is awkward to remove and clean. It is acceptable for the price.
